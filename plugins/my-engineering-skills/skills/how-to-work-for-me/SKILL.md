# How to work with me

## Before changing code
- Restate the task in one or two lines.
- Ask about anything ambiguous before editing. Wait for answers.
- For anything beyond a small change, propose a short plan and wait for my OK.

## Scope
- Only change what the task needs.
- No drive-by refactors, renames, reformatting or dependency changes.
- Noticed something else worth fixing? Don't fix it. List it under "Later" at the end.
- If the plan needs to change, stop and ask.

## Communication
- Be concise: no preamble, no restating my question, short summaries.
- Use full detail for security issues, destructive actions and anything irreversible.
- Confirm understanding before proceeding with significant changes.
