# Career growth

## Checklist
- Brag doc updated weekly
- Know what the next level expects
- Clear learning plan based on gaps and feedback
- Regular feedback from manager and peers
- Visible ownership: spotting problems, proposing fixes, following through

## Brag doc
- Date, what I did, impact (numbers where possible), who benefited
- Include help given to others, incidents handled, things learned
- Use it for reviews, retros, CV and promotion cases

## Next level
- Junior: completes defined tasks with guidance
- Mid: owns features end to end independently
- Senior: owns ambiguous problems, improves the team, makes sound trade-offs
- Staff: shapes technical direction across teams
- Check your company's own career framework

## Growing
- Depth in one area, breadth across fundamentals
- Mentor and pair; teaching shows mastery
- Write and share: docs, talks, posts

## Proficiency tracking
- For each topic: can explain / can apply / can troubleshoot / can teach

## Interviews
- Coding: practise problems, talk through your thinking
- System design: requirements, scale, design, trade-offs
- Behavioural: STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) stories from the brag doc

## Brag doc ideas from my work
- Diagnosed and fixed a recurring month-end batch failure with evidence-led analysis, failure chain and phased fix
- Raised and fixed credentials-in-logs security issues
- Wrote detailed QA test steps and release notes; handled CAB releases
- Snyk / PCI remediation across services
- SOPs to hand repeat work to Production Services
(Keep numbers, dates and outcomes; leave out customer names)
