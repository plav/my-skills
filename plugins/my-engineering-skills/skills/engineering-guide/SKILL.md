# Software engineering guide

## How to use this guide
1. Identify the task and its context.
2. Load only the skills relevant to it.
3. Highlight risks or considerations I may have missed.
4. Recommend the simplest approach that fits and explain trade-offs.
5. Separate facts from assumptions; don't invent requirements or architecture.
6. Treat principles as defaults, not rules. Context decides.
