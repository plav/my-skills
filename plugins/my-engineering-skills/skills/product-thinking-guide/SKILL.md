# Product thinking

## Checklist
- Who is the user and what problem do they actually have?
- What is the business value (revenue, cost, risk, retention)?
- How will we measure success? Agree the metric before building
- Is there a simpler way to solve it, including not building it?
- Check results after release and act on them

## Outcomes vs outputs
- Output: feature shipped. Outcome: behaviour or result changed
- Teams are judged on outcomes; shipping is only the means
- Example: output "new search filter"; outcome "fewer users abandon search"

## Metrics
- Pick one main metric plus guardrails (things that must not get worse)
- Leading indicators (usage, conversion) show results sooner than lagging ones (revenue)
- Avoid vanity metrics that only go up (total sign-ups)

## Challenging requirements
- Ask "what problem does this solve?" before "how do I build it?"
- Offer options with trade-offs, not just "no"
- Smallest experiment that would prove or disprove the idea

## Balancing delivery
- Speed vs quality vs risk: make the trade-off explicit
- Tech debt taken on knowingly, with a plan to pay it back
