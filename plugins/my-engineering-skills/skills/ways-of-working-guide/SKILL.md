# Ways of working

## Checklist
- Ask questions early; don't stay stuck alone for long
- Share work before it's "finished"
- Communicate progress, risks and blockers proactively
- Keep commitments or flag early when you can't
- Help others: reviews, pairing, answering questions

## Agile ceremonies
- Standup: progress, plan, blockers; keep short
- Planning: agree sprint goal and what fits
- Refinement: get upcoming work ready
- Review/demo: show working software, get feedback
- Retro: improve one or two things each time

## Communication
- Async by default (written updates), meetings when discussion is needed
- Status update: done, next, blockers, risks
- Tailor detail to the audience (technical vs non-technical)

## Collaboration
- Pairing and mobbing for hard problems and knowledge sharing
- Disagree with evidence and trade-offs; then commit to the decision
- Credit others' work

## Feedback
- Specific, timely, about behaviour and impact
- Ask for feedback regularly, not only at reviews

## Lessons from my work
- Lots of work is done in 1:1 Teams chats: summarise decisions back into the Jira ticket
- When several tickets could release together, write a short doc of open items and risks, and consider decoupling
- Agree changes to acceptance criteria with the reporter and note it in the ticket
